## 4 Estimates

The scope is deliberately narrow: articles with a text layer, converted for reading by a language model. Within that scope the results are stable across the publishers we tried. Outside it, users should expect to check the output before trusting it. A second reviewer asked for timings; conversion runs at a few pages per second on a laptop.

The effect of density is −0.10 and that of income is −0.09 with p <.05 in 2 of the 3 models.
